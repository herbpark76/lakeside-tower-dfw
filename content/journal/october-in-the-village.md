October is the month Lakeside Village throws its best parties. Three of them are on the calendar this month, and none require a car.

## Hopptoberfest at Hoppin' Grapevine

Saturday, October 3, from noon. A free stein giveaway for the first fifteen through the door, a stein-holding contest, German food and Oktoberfest beers on tap. Free tickets on Eventbrite.

## Oktoberfest block party at The Tavern at Lakeside

Saturday, October 10, noon to ten. Free to attend, with $50 VIP seats that get you private bars in front of the stage. [Reserve a VIP table](https://tavernlakesideflowermound.com/lakeside-village-oktoberfest/#reserve).

## Hoppin' Grapevine turns two

Also Saturday, October 10. Live music in the afternoon, karaoke at night, and giveaways throughout the day.

Plans change — check each venue before you go.
