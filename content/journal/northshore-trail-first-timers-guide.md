Most trails start with a drive. This one starts with a four-minute walk.

The Lakeside Village trailhead sits on the Lakeside Boardwalk, just west of the tower near Edgemere and Loma Alta. From there the Northshore Trail runs roughly twenty-two miles in seven loops along the north shore of Lake Grapevine, between Rockledge Park to the east and Twin Coves Park to the west.

## What to expect

- **It's a real trail.** Single-track, rooty and rocky in places, rated intermediate to challenging. The western loops are the hardest, with the steepest climbs.
- **It's shared.** Hikers, runners and mountain bikers use it together. No horses.
- **Rain matters.** The trail closes to bikes when the ground is wet; walking stays open. Check conditions before you ride after a storm.
- **Dogs are welcome** on a leash.

## A good first outing

Start at the boardwalk and head out on the nearest loop. Turn around when you've had enough; the lake is on your side the whole way back. Early mornings are cooler and quieter, and in summer they're the only sensible time to go.

## Before and after

If you need gear, Trailhead Running Supply and Cadence Cyclery are both in the Village, about a thirteen-minute walk up the parkway. Epic Gelato & Craft Coffee and Starbucks are a few minutes farther, for the walk home.
