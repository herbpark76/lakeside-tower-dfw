There is a particular pleasure in walking to dinner and walking home again. No valet, no parking lot, no one deciding who drives.

Lakeside Village begins a short walk up the parkway from the tower. Here is what's there, by how far you'll go.

## Twelve minutes

**The Tavern at Lakeside** for an American menu and a full bar. **Sakhuu Thai** next door. **Clink Wine Bar & Bites** when the evening only needs a glass and something small. **Touring Chocolatier** for afterward.

## Thirteen minutes

**Mena's Tex-Mex Grill & Cantina**, **Mio Nonno Trattoria** for Italian, and **Branded Bowls** for something lighter. **Flurry's Lakeside** for dessert.

## Fourteen minutes

**1845 Taste Texas**, **Vivo Wood Fire Pizzeria**, and **Egg Farm Cafe** for breakfast and brunch. **Epic Gelato & Craft Coffee** is here too.

## Fifteen to sixteen minutes

**Hanaya Sushi, Hibachi & Ramen**, **Del Campo Empanadas**, and **Lakeside Donuts** along Long Prairie Road — and the **Moviehouse & Eatery**, where dinner comes to your seat.

## A note

Menus, hours and tenants change. Check before you go, and see the [walkability map](/location) for directions from the front door.
